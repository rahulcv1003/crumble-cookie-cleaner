Changelog

0.2.0, 27 October 2026

  • A redesigned popup, settings page and setup flow, with refined light and dark themes and a locally bundled Inter typeface.
  • Contextual help explains what settings and cleanup actions do before you choose them. Use hover, keyboard focus or the question-mark buttons; Escape dismisses help.
  • Improved narrow-window layouts and clearer warnings about cookie Undo and irreversible site-data deletion. Cleanup rules and permissions are unchanged.

0.1.0, 15 October 2026

  • First release: cleaning on tab close, domain change and browser start; Keep and Keep until restart lists with Cookie AutoDelete's rule syntax.
  • Cleans first-party, third-party and partitioned cookies, LocalStorage, IndexedDB, Cache Storage and Service Workers.
  • Setup, popup, settings, cleanup log, 2-minute undo, and import from Cookie AutoDelete.
