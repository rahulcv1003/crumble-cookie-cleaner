Delete cookies when you close the tab

Crumble deletes a site's cookies and site data shortly after you close its last tab. The sites you choose to keep stay logged in.

  • Close a tab, and the site forgets you. After a short delay (15 seconds by default) its cookies, LocalStorage, IndexedDB, Cache Storage and Service Workers are deleted, including third-party and partitioned cookies.
  • Keep the sites you trust. Sites on Keep are never cleaned. Sites on Keep until restart stay until the browser next starts.
  • Nothing leaves your browser. No account, no analytics, no servers. Undo restores the last cleanup's cookies for 2 minutes.
